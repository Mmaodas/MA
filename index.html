Professional Profile

Healthcare IT & Digital Health Professional | Clinical Information Systems | Business Analysis | Project Implementation

I am a healthcare technology professional with over 7 years of experience supporting healthcare information systems, electronic medical records, digital health initiatives, clinical workflows, and end-users across healthcare environments. My experience combines healthcare operations, information technology, business analysis, project coordination, and occupational health and safety.

In my current role with Nova Scotia Health's One Person One Record (OPOR) program, I support the implementation and adoption of a province-wide Clinical Information System (CIS). I work directly with frontline healthcare professionals to provide at-the-elbow support, resolve system and workflow-related issues, support go-live activities, and help users effectively adopt new clinical technologies. I collaborate with clinical, technical, administrative, and project teams to identify issues, improve workflows, and provide practical solutions that support safe and effective healthcare delivery.

Previously, I worked in healthcare technology implementation and support, including electronic medical record deployment across multiple healthcare facilities. My experience includes end-user training, application support, workflow analysis, stakeholder engagement, troubleshooting, implementation planning, and go-live support. I have also led and mentored technical support teams while working closely with healthcare professionals and senior stakeholders.

My professional interests include healthcare information systems, digital transformation, clinical and business systems analysis, healthcare data and analytics, process improvement, project management, change management, and technology-enabled patient care. I am particularly interested in using data, technology, and process improvement to strengthen healthcare operations, improve user experience, and support better access to care.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Biochemistry, a Post-Baccalaureate Diploma in Occupational Health and Safety, and a Master's degree in Healthcare Management/E-Health Management. This multidisciplinary background allows me to understand healthcare from both clinical and operational perspectives while contributing effectively to technology and transformation initiatives.

Core Areas: Healthcare IT, Clinical Information Systems, Digital Health, Business Analysis, Requirements Analysis, Workflow Optimization, Data Analysis, Project Coordination, Go-Live Support, End-User Training, Change Management, Stakeholder Engagement, Process Improvement, Healthcare Operations, and Occupational Health & Safety.
